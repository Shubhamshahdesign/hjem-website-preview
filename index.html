About Hjem

A small Danish café on a quiet Kensington corner. Buns twisted with cardamom and cinnamon, dark seeded rye, loose-leaf tea in cast-iron pots, and coffee from half past seven on weekdays.

Two cafés in Kensington with the same bakes and the same hours.

A day at Hjem

From the first coffee to the last bun.

The menu

A taste of what's on the counter. Prices are on the board in the café and the bakes change with the season.

  • Cardamom knot

    Soft dough twisted with cardamom sugar and baked dark at the edges.

  • Cinnamon bun

    A tight cinnamon swirl finished with pearl sugar.

  • Poppy seed pastry

    Flaky layers under a thick crust of poppy seeds.

  • Chocolate cookie

    Dark, soft in the middle, good with a flat white.

  • Avocado on rye

    Dark Danish rye, crushed avocado, pickled red onion and chives.

  • Potato on rye

    Sliced potato, chives and black pepper on buttered rye.

  • Avocado and egg

    Seeded rye with avocado, toasted seeds and a fried egg.

  • Waffle

    Golden waffle with sliced banana and chocolate chips.

  • Espresso bar

    Espresso, americano, flat white, cappuccino and latte. Oat milk on request.

  • Green smoothie

    Blended to order, served in a tumbler.

  • Elderflower posh pop

    Sparkling elderflower from Breckland Orchard.

  • Loose-leaf tea, by the pot

    Organic teas from Cocoon Tea Artisans, brewed in cast iron.

  • Black

    English Breakfast, Earl Grey

  • Oolong

    Oolong, Darjeeling Oolong

  • Green and white

    Green Jasmine, Green Quince, White Tea

  • Herbal

    Herbal Blend
